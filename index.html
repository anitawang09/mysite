Powering Festivals, Fueling Risk?

Enhancing festival safety through strategic communication & behavioral change

With the Amsterdam-Amstelland Safety Region & Fire Department, our team investigated why fire safety around lithium-ion batteries at festivals is treated as a compliance hurdle rather than a shared responsibility. Drawing on the Theory of Planned Behavior, Social Norms Theory, and Fogg's Behavior Model, I helped design a user-centred digital platform that repositions firefighters as proactive knowledge partners rather than regulators — making it easier for festival organizers to collaborate on safety and sustainable energy use.

  • Behavioral theory
  • Strategic communication
  • Platform design

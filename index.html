01

Powering Festivals, Fueling Risk?

With the Amsterdam-Amstelland fire brigade

Festivals increasingly run on big lithium batteries, which can catch fire. The Amsterdam-Amstelland fire brigade wanted organisers to come to them for advice early — but organisers mostly saw them as the people who hand out rules. Our team asked both sides what was getting in the way, then designed a website that gives organisers practical safety guidance and an easy way to get in touch, so the fire brigade becomes a helpful partner instead of an inspector.

  • Interviews
  • Communication strategy
  • Website design

Partner: Brandweer Amsterdam-Amstelland ↗

02

Do wind-powered sails really save fuel?

With MARIN, the Dutch maritime research institute

Some cargo ships now carry sails to cut their fuel use. Shipping companies want to know whether it's worth the investment. I compared 40 ships with and without sails, measuring fuel and emissions per mile travelled rather than totals, so that big ships on long routes didn't skew the picture. Ships with sails did slightly better, but the gap was too small to call it proven — an honest answer that matters more than a flattering one. I also gathered thousands of online comments from people in the industry to see what really holds them back: cost, subsidies, rules, and whether ports are ready.

  • Python
  • Statistics
  • Data collection

Code & analysis: github.com/lotjas/Popeyes ↗

03

Helping a second-hand shop run better

With Kringloopwinkel De Lokatie, Amsterdam

A charity shop that resells donated goods wanted to know why day-to-day work kept getting stuck. I interviewed staff, volunteers and management, sorted what they told me into recurring themes, and handed back a short list of practical changes — the problems that came up most often, and what to do about each one.

  • Interviews
  • Theme analysis
  • Recommendations
